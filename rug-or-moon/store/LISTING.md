**Full description:**

Rug or Moon is the 5-second "should I ape this?" check for Solana tokens.

Paste any token address and get an instant, plain-English safety read:

🚩 **Rug red flags** — is the mint authority still active (creator can print
unlimited supply)? Can they freeze your wallet? Do a handful of wallets control
most of the supply? Is the liquidity thin enough to pull in one transaction? Is
it brand new? Does the volume smell like wash trading?

🌙 **Alpha signals** — is tracked smart money already holding? Is there real buy
pressure, or are sellers heading for the exit?

🔥 **Trending board** — open the app to today's trending tokens, auto-scanned
and sorted safest-first, so you see the gems and the rugs at a glance.

👀 **Watchlist + alerts** — save tokens and get alerted when a watched token's
safety drops, its risk tier downgrades, tracked smart money exits, or a new red
flag appears.

✦ **Seeker Edition exclusive** — installing from the Solana dApp Store unlocks
**unlimited watchlist** slots. This app ships only on the dApp Store — it's built
for Seeker.

Everything is a heuristic estimate from public on-chain data. Rug or Moon is
**not financial advice** and not a guarantee — a high score isn't a green light
and a low score isn't proof of a scam. Always do your own research.

No account. No personal data. No tracking. (See the privacy policy.)
