Rug or Moon

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 July 2026

The short version

Rug or Moon has no accounts and collects no personal information. The only thing it sends anywhere is the token address you choose to scan — and that goes to our own server purely to look up public blockchain data. Your watchlist stays on your device. We don't track you, run ads, or sell data.

What we collect

Personal information: none. We don't ask for your name, email, wallet address, or any account. You can use the entire app without identifying yourself.

Information you send when you scan

When you scan a token, the app sends the token address you typed to our own server (hosted on Vercel) so it can fetch that token's public on-chain and market data. This request is used only to generate your result and is not linked to any identity. We do not build a profile of what you scan.

Your watchlist and settings

Tokens you save to your watchlist are stored locally on your own device (in your browser's storage). They are never uploaded to us. When the app re-checks your watchlist, it scans each saved token the same way as above — sending only the token address. If you clear your browser data or remove the app, your watchlist is gone.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, alerts about your watched tokens are generated on your device by your browser. We do not operate a push-notification server and do not receive your device token.

Third-party data sources

To produce a scan, our server queries public blockchain and market-data providers. These services see the token address (not you) as part of a normal server request:

  • DexScreener — public market data (price, liquidity, volume).
  • Helius — public Solana RPC (token authorities and top holders).
  • Birdeye — trending-token data (only if enabled by the operator).

These are read-only lookups of public information. We never share personal data with them because we don't collect any.

Analytics and advertising

We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. There are no tracking cookies.

The "Buy on Jupiter" button

The app can link out to Jupiter to swap a token. Tapping it opens Jupiter in a new tab; any trade happens on Jupiter under their terms and privacy policy, with your own wallet. Rug or Moon never has access to your funds, keys, or wallet.

Children

This app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
